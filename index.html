Our Story

About the Bags

Every piece in our collection carries the legacy of Louis Vuitton — a name synonymous with French luxury since 1854. From the iconic Speedy, originally designed for Audrey Hepburn, to the timeless Neverfull and the elegant Alma, each bag represents over 170 years of craftsmanship, innovation, and enduring style.

We carefully curate only authentic pieces, ensuring every item meets the highest standards of quality and authenticity. Whether you choose the classic Speedy 25, the versatile Neverfull MM, the Boulogne, or the Noé BB — you're not just carrying a bag, you're carrying a piece of fashion history.

Right here in Tankulan, Manolo Fortich, Bukidnon, we bring this world-class heritage closer to you. Genuine luxury, personalized service, and a passion for elegance — that's Mejaiii's LV Bag Collections.

MJ Lauron and Caleb Villafuerte
Founders of the Luxury Collection

Where It All Began

A passionate lover of elegance and authentic luxury, MJ and Caleb established this collection in Tankulan, Manolo Fortich, Bukidnon — bringing world-class luxury closer to Manolo Fortich.

It all began on December 17, 2024, with a simple dream shared by MJ and Caleb: to bring the beauty of luxury and timeless fashion closer to their local community. What started as a passion for elegant designs and high-quality bags gradually grew into a vision of making authentic luxury more accessible in Manolo, Fortich, Bukidnon. Through dedication, careful selection, and a commitment to quality, they began building a collection that reflects sophistication, confidence, and lasting style. This date marked the beginning of their journey toward turning a shared passion into something meaningful for every customer.

With a vision to make genuine luxury accessible in the province, every piece is carefully selected for quality and authenticity. "Luxury is not just about the bag — it's about confidence, timeless style, and making every client feel special."
